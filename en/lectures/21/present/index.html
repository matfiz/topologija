Lecture 21

Gauge fields: connections, curvature and holonomy

A bundle has local frames, but differentiating their coefficients depends on the frame. A connection corrects this derivative and specifies parallel transport. We derive its change law and show how curvature measures transport around a small loop. A flat connection can still have nontrivial transport around a hole. The Aharonov–Bohm phase is this effect for a charged particle.
