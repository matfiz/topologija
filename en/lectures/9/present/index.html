Lecture 9

Vector fields and flows

A smooth vector field prescribes a velocity at every point of a manifold. Its integral curves describe the resulting motion. Comparing a second field along that motion gives the Lie bracket. We prove the connection between a zero bracket and commuting complete flows, then compute the rotation fields and their signs. Left translation turns a tangent vector at the identity of a Lie group into a vector field. This defines the group’s Lie algebra and its exponential map.

Summary

A vector field prescribes an ordinary differential equation in every chart. Uniqueness gives the flow group law, and compact support gives completeness. The Lie derivative of a field uses backward push-forward and equals its bracket with the generating field. Complete fields have zero bracket exactly when their flows commute. Linear fields AxAxAx have the negative matrix commutator, whereas left-invariant matrix fields gAgAgA have the positive one. Lecture 10 introduces forms and their pullback under arbitrary smooth maps.
