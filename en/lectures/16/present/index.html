Lecture 16

Connections and geodesics

Differentiating a tangent field involves both its components and its coordinate basis. A connection accounts for this change. A metric selects one connection by compatibility and zero torsion. Its parallel transport preserves inner products, and its geodesics have parallel velocity.

16.2 The Levi-Civita connection

We derive the connection from metric compatibility and zero torsion. The cyclic sum in Koszul’s formula determines it uniquely.

Definition 16.5. A torsion-free metric connection is called the Levi-Civita connection.

Summary

A nondegenerate metric determines the torsion-free metric connection by Koszul’s formula. Parallel transport solves a linear ODE and preserves the metric. Geodesics have parallel velocity and constant metric norm. Killing fields give conserved pairings with that velocity. For a bi-invariant metric the identity geodesics are one-parameter subgroups. Compact averaging and the cited Hopf–Rinow theorem make the group exponential onto for compact connected groups. Lecture 17 computes curvature from this connection.
