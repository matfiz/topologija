Lecture 19

Lorentzian geometry and general relativity

A Lorentzian metric specifies elapsed time and the paths of freely falling particles. Einstein’s equation relates its curvature to matter. We recover Newton’s acceleration in a weak field and verify the Schwarzschild vacuum metric. Its conserved quantities then reduce a massive particle’s motion to an orbit equation.

Unit 19A Motion in a given metric

19.1 Lorentzian metrics and proper time

Definition 19.1 (Lorentzian spacetime). A Lorentzian spacetime is a smooth four-dimensional manifold with a metric of signature (+,−,−,−)(+,-,-,-)(+,−,−,−).

At each point an orthonormal frame identifies the metric with η=diag⁡(1,−1,−1,−1)\Minkowski=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)η=diag(1,−1,−1,−1). This recalls the flat interval in Symmetries in Physics, Lecture 17, Minkowski space from physics. Here the metric may vary from point to point. Assume the spacetime is oriented, so its metric volume form is defined.

Definition 19.2 (Causal type and time orientation). A nonzero tangent vector vvv is timelike if g(v,v)>0\metric(v,v)>0g(v,v)>0, null if g(v,v)=0\metric(v,v)=0g(v,v)=0, and spacelike if g(v,v)<0\metric(v,v)<0g(v,v)<0. Physical input.

The physical free-fall postulate says that an unforced test particle follows a timelike geodesic of the Levi-Civita connection. A light ray follows a null geodesic, using an affine parameter in place of proper time. These laws describe unforced structureless test bodies and light in the geometric-optics approximation. Universal free fall motivates assigning test-body trajectories to spacetime geometry. It does not derive the metric geodesic law. An ideal clock measures proper time along its worldline.

The existence and equation of these geodesics are the mathematical results of Lecture 16.

Summary

A weak static Lorentzian metric gives Newton’s equation for slow free fall. The cited Einstein–Hilbert variation gives Einstein’s equation with positive 8πGNTμν8\pi\NewtonG\stress_{\mu\nu}8πGN​Tμν​ in our conventions. Contracting the second Bianchi identity gives covariant conservation. The Schwarzschild exterior has zero Ricci tensor and nonzero full curvature.
