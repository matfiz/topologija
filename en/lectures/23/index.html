Lecture 23

Yang–Mills theory and instantons

What this lecture does

Lecture 21 supplied the geometry of a gauge field. We now choose an action and derive its field equation. For an SU(2)\SU(2)SU(2) connection extending over the four-sphere, the curvature also gives an integer. This integer bounds the action and identifies solutions of a first-order equation. The BPST field fixes the orientation and normalization in a concrete example.
