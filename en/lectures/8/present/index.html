Lecture 8

Tangent spaces, Lie groups and homogeneous spaces

A tangent vector records the initial velocity of a curve without choosing an ambient Euclidean space. Coordinate changes relate its components by their Jacobian matrices. We use these vectors to differentiate maps and describe the infinitesimal directions of matrix groups. A transitive smooth action of a Lie group presents a manifold as a quotient by the subgroup fixing a point. The sphere and the qubit state space give two concrete quotient descriptions.

8.4 The tangent bundle

A moving point has both a position and a velocity at that position. We collect these pairs in one space so that the velocity can vary along a curve.

Self-check.

State in one sentence what a tangent vector is and why we introduced it. Then describe a point of TS1\tangentbundle\sphere1TS1 and what its projection retains.

Answer.

A tangent vector is a class of curves through one point with the same coordinate velocity, introduced to define velocity without an ambient space. The chain rule makes equality of those velocities independent of the chart. A point of TS1\tangentbundle\sphere1TS1 is a circle position together with one tangent velocity there. The projection retains the position.

The tangent space at the identity of a smooth group describes the velocities of curves of symmetries that start at the identity.

8.7 The sphere and the Hopf map

The standard action of SO(n+1)\SO(n+1)SO(n+1) on Sn\sphere nSn is transitive. Given a unit vector, extend it to an oriented orthonormal basis by Gram–Schmidt, recalled from Linear Algebra for Physics. Use it as the last column of an orthogonal matrix, completing the other columns with positive orientation. That matrix sends the last coordinate unit vector to the chosen point. The stabiliser consists of matrices with a block in SO(n)\SO(n)SO(n) on its orthogonal complement and a last diagonal entry 111. The group O(n+1)\Orth(n+1)O(n+1) is a regular level set by Lecture 7, so it is a Lie group by Proposition 8.16. Its determinant-one part is open and hence is also a Lie group. The sphere action is the restriction of the polynomial map (g,p)↦gp(g,p)\mapsto gp(g,p)↦gp, so it is smooth. Summary

Tangent vectors are velocity classes. A chart identifies them with coordinate columns, and its Jacobian gives their transformation law. Differentials send curve velocities to image velocities and satisfy the chain rule. The tangent space of a regular level set is the kernel of the constraint’s derivative. Closed stabilisers give smooth quotient manifolds by cited theorems. The rotation and qubit actions identify the sphere with SO(3)/SO(2)\SO(3)/\SO(2)SO(3)/SO(2) and SU(2)/U(1)\SU(2)/\U(1)SU(2)/U(1) respectively. Lecture 9 studies curves whose velocities are prescribed at every point by a vector field.
