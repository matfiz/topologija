Lecture 2 · Compactness, connectedness and gluing

Self-check.

Prove that S1\sphere1S1 and [0,1][0,1][0,1] are not homeomorphic by removing one point. Explain why deleting an endpoint of the interval is not a useful test.

Show answer

Answer.

Delete the midpoint of [0,1][0,1][0,1]. The two resulting half-intervals give a separation. Deleting any point from the circle leaves a space homeomorphic to R\RR. Rotate that point to the north pole, then use Lecture 1’s stereographic projection. The line is path-connected by straight segments. A homeomorphism would restrict to a homeomorphism between these two punctured spaces, which is impossible. Deleting an endpoint leaves a path-connected half-open interval, so connectedness would not distinguish them.

Lecture 6 · Homology and the Euler characteristic

Self-check.

Show answer

Answer.
